# Interpretive Crosswalk to Selected Governance Concepts

This analytical mapping identifies possible connections between selected external concepts and this working profile. It does not establish compliance, conformity, certification, control effectiveness, model credibility, or regulator acceptance. It is not an exhaustive requirements assessment. Adopters must determine applicability and obtain the relevant authoritative materials.

Sources inspected on 2026-09-09. The EU row identifies provisions in the original 2024 text; it makes no assertion about current applicability dates or later amendments. The ISO row uses the public overview, without claiming clause-level review of the full standard.

| External source | Relevant concept | Profile/RGDS mechanism | Evidence it may generate | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0, GOVERN 2 and 3.2](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) | Accountability and differentiated human-AI roles | Covenants 2 and 4; assigned ownership and review | Recorded responsibilities and reviewer findings | Does not demonstrate empowered personnel, effective oversight, or implementation of the full RMF |
| [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 public overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | Organizational AI management system | Documented participation rules and review procedures | Policy and decision-review records | Conceptual connection only; no clause-level conformity, management-system audit, or certification established |
| [EU Regulation 2024/1689, original text, Articles 13 and 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) | Transparency and human oversight of high-risk systems | Disclosure, review/rejection, declared human authority | Tool-purpose disclosures and review records | A record does not establish provision of required system information, effective oversight measures, or legal compliance; applicability needs separate legal assessment |
| [FDA January 2025 draft guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/considerations-use-artificial-intelligence-support-regulatory-decision-making-drug-and-biological) | AI model credibility for a particular regulatory context of use | Tool-purpose, source, limitation, and review documentation | Traceable context and governance records | Draft, non-binding, not for implementation; recording decision provenance does not establish model credibility or adequacy for a context of use |

The FDA source concerns AI producing information or data for regulatory decisions about drug safety, effectiveness, or quality. This profile does not assert that general drafting or workflow assistance is automatically within that scope, or that its covenants implement the draft's credibility assessment framework.

The prior ARAF v3.0 row is withdrawn from the active crosswalk: the repository did not supply an identifiable versioned source and provision that could support the claimed satisfaction. Historical mention remains in the changelog. Reinstatement requires an inspectable source, precise concept, and explicit boundary.

These are author-derived interpretations of external concepts. None of the source organizations has reviewed or accepted this mapping. See [status and limitations](../README.md).
